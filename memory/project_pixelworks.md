## Technology Stack

### TrueCut 3.2 (current production)
- Fast throughput — primary tool for all commercial work
- Problem: output files cannot be ingested by TrueCut 9 for editing
- Will remain in use for 9+ months until Flash ships

### TrueCut 9
- Has motion editing tools for problem shots
- Problem: slow throughput (can't do a whole film efficiently)
- Requires re-processing 3.2 shots to use its editing tools

### TrueCut Flash (in development — target Dec 31, 2026)
- Replaces 3.2 as primary production tool
- Key requirement (Todd's mandate): output files must be editable in TrueCut 9
- Must be faster throughput than 3.2
- Plugin target: DaVinci Resolve first, then Nuke and FilmLight (Baselight)
- Engineering team historically built for Cameron/Lightstorm — needs shift to 90% market

### Flash PM Status (as of July 14 2026 team call)
- Team received ass chewing for presenting theoretical Gantt charts
- Todd's mandate: weekly reviews, named owners per line item, end dates are fixed, use AI for PM
- One free pass given; no more theoretical timelines
